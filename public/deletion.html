Account Deletion

Delete your account

Two ways to do it. Both are permanent, and both are complete — there's no hidden copy kept somewhere.

In the app

The fastest way, if you can still open Stillform:

  • Open Settings.
  • Open Privacy & contact.
  • Tap Delete my account, read what it removes, and confirm.

You'll see a receipt when it's done.

By email

If you can't reach the app, write to us from the email address on your account:

ARAembersllc@proton.me

Put “Delete my account” in the subject. We'll delete the account and its records, and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your account — the sign-in itself, removed from our server.
  • Your records with us — subscription status and anything tied to your account.
  • Your practice history — every session, note, and setting. In Stillform this lives on your own device, so deleting in the app clears it directly; an email deletion removes your account and server records, and you can clear the app's local data by uninstalling.

One thing to check

If you subscribed through the App Store or Google Play, cancel that subscription in the store as well — app stores bill separately from your account here, and deleting your account doesn't stop a store subscription.

Deletion in daylight. You were never trapped here.
